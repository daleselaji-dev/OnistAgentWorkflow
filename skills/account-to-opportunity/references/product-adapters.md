# Product Adapter Reference

## Industrial 3D printing
Jobs: prototypes, tooling/jigs/fixtures, rapid tooling, high-mix low-volume, replacement parts. Strong company fit does not compensate for technical-envelope failure.

## CLO
Fashion product development, technical design, fit/sampling and digital product creation.

## Marvelous Designer
Game, VFX, animation and CG character clothing / cloth-asset pipeline.

## CLOFAB / zFab
Fabric mills, suppliers, brands and material teams that need repeatable fabric digitization / digital-material delivery.

## CLO-SET
Organizations with growing 3D asset volume, version/review complexity, multi-team/vendor collaboration, web tech pack or product-lifecycle workflow needs.

One account may match multiple adapters; qualification is adapter-specific.
