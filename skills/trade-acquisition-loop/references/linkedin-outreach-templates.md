# LinkedIn 触达模板参考

**硬前提**:LinkedIn 消息只能人工执行(平台条款禁自动化),agent 只产草稿并标注"人工发送"。每人只发 1 次连接请求;7 天内 ≤2 条消息;连接备注 ≤300 字符且不 pitch;首条消息 ≤500 字符、不带链接、不 pitch。

## 两段式:连接请求 → 首条消息

### 连接请求备注(≤300 字符)句段职责

| 句段 | 职责 |
|---|---|
| 第 1 句 | 你是谁 + 与对方角色相关的理由(不是"想卖你东西") |
| 第 2 句 | 具体观察(招聘/项目/演讲/文章)——证明不是群发 |
| 第 3 句 | 低承诺的连接理由("交流笔记"而非"给你演示") |

骨架:
```text
Hi {{NAME}} — I work with {{ROLE_PEER_GROUP}} on {{DOMAIN}}.
{{SPECIFIC_OBSERVATION_ABOUT_THEIR_TEAM}} caught my eye.
Would value connecting to exchange notes on {{TOPIC}}.
```

### 首条消息(连接接受后,≤500 字符,无链接,不 pitch)

| 句段 | 职责 |
|---|---|
| 第 1 句 | 谢连接,一句即可 |
| 第 2 句 | FACT:可查证观察 + 它通常意味着什么(条件式) |
| 第 3 句 | 真诚的开放式问题(QUESTION)——目标是验证 pain/所有权,不是推销 |

骨架:
```text
Thanks for connecting, {{NAME}}. I saw {{OBSERVABLE_FACT}} — usually a
sign a team is {{CONDITIONAL_INTERPRETATION}}. Curious how far along
that is on your side: {{OPEN_QUESTION}}? No pitch — genuinely
interested in how you sequence it.
```

## 工业 3DP 示例

### 好例子(连接备注)

```text
Hi Stefan — I work with manufacturing engineering teams on in-house
additive tooling. Your team's posting for a fixtures engineer caught
my eye. Would value connecting to exchange notes on tooling workflows.
```

### 好例子(首条消息)

```text
Thanks for connecting, Stefan. The fixtures-engineer role plus the new
Stuttgart line suggests tooling volume is climbing — usually when
external machine-shop queues start to hurt. How is your team handling
fixture turnaround today: in-house, outsourced, or mixed?
```

### 坏例子(首条消息)

```text
Thanks for accepting! We are the leading manufacturer of industrial 3D
printers. Check out our case studies: https://example.com/cases — we
guarantee you will save 30% on tooling. When are you free for a demo
this week?
```

为什么坏:`leading manufacturer`(HYPE)、首条带链接(门禁拒)、`guarantee ... save 30%`(ROI 承诺)、首条即硬约 demo(pitch-first)、全程没有对方上下文。

## CLO 示例

### 好例子(首条消息)

```text
Thanks for connecting, Sofia. I saw Blue Atelier is hiring a CLO
designer — usually a sign a team is moving sampling into 3D. Curious
how far along that shift is on your side: are fit sessions still
driven by physical samples? No pitch — genuinely interested in how
you sequence it.
```

## 跟进触点差异

- **T2-on-channel(≥4 天,7 天窗口内总数 ≤2)**:分享一个与对方角色直接相关的证据资产(会议演讲、行业数据、同行案例的机制描述),此时可带 1 个链接;一句话说明"为什么与你相关",不追问上一条。
- **T3-on-channel**:轻量收尾 + 转介绍出口:`If 3D sampling isn't on your plate, who on the team owns it? Happy to stop here otherwise.`
- LinkedIn 收尾后,序列回 Email 完成 T4 收尾信。

## 约会议(LinkedIn 版)

```text
Sounds worth a proper look. 20 minutes: {{SLOT_1}} or {{SLOT_2}}
({{RECIPIENT_TZ}})? I'll bring {{OUR_ROLE}}; useful if {{THEIR_ROLE}}
joins. Agenda in one line: {{ONE_LINE_AGENDA}}.
```

对方接受后立即转 Email 发日历邀请与确认包(LinkedIn 不承载会议资产)。
